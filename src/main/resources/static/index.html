    Impact & how ranking works

    Aligned with SDG 3 (Good Health & Well-being), target 3.8: access to essential medicines for all.

    How results are ranked

    Every report gets four scores between 0 and 100, combined with fixed weights. Reports that show stock always come before "out of stock" ones; the score orders each group.

    • Availability: available = 100, low stock = 50, out of stock = 0.
    • Freshness: halves every 3 hours since the outlet last updated. A report from 3 days ago scores almost 0.
    • Verification: audited outlets score 100, others 50.
    • Distance: 100 at your location, falling to 0 at the edge of the search radius.
